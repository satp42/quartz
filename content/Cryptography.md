Cryptography is a technique for securing information and communications through the use of codes. The benefits of this allows for security measures to be tied to the data that is being traveled through messaging itself, that way it can be passed through in a secure manner even when the pipelines are adversarial.

Most cryptosystems begin with an unencrypted message (aka plaintext) and is encrypted into an undecipherable code known as the cryptotext, that must be deciphered through the same key that made the encryption algorithm.
