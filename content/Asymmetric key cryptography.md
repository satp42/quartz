This is a form of cryptography that has the sender's public key which is used for encryption, and the receiver's private key is used for decryption. Essentially there are different public and private keys. This form of cryptography favors encryption over simplicity.

There are several examples of asymmetric key cryptography including:
- [[RSA encryption]]
- [[Elliptic curve cryptography]]
