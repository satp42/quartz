New advance in AI capabilities allow for new capabilities in workflows that can be automated. The general paradigm of [[Large Language Models]] as a form of copilot assistance has now turned into agents that work concurrently with you. This is broadly due to core advancements in the following:
- [[Generative video will become a new computing paradigm in 2026]]
- [[Reducto Document Parsing Framework]] as the epitome of better document parsing
- [[Browser automation as a critical tool for agent frameworks]]
- [[Voice and conversational agents opened a new domain of automation]]
The real unlock comes from **combination**. When you can parse documents AND navigate legacy systems AND hold conversations, you can now automate entire end-to-end workflows that were previously impossible. This isn't incremental; it's categorical. The beauty of this is that it enables new AI companies to prosper because [[Workflow ontology creates moats through accumulated operational knowledge]] that lock in customers to their products.
