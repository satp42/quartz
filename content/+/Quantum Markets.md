Quantum Markets could revolutionize university admissions by evaluating thousands of applicant proposals simultaneously.

  

Imagine a university admissions process powered by Quantum Markets. Instead of the traditional, rigid system where each applicant is evaluated in isolation or by a small committee, the admissions office could open a quantum market for each incoming class. Here’s how it could work:

  

**Quantum Market for University Admissions**

- **Decision Goal:** Select the set of applicants that will maximize the university’s long-term success (measured by metrics like future donations, research output, diversity, or social impact).
- **Proposals:** Each applicant (or even groups of applicants) becomes a proposal in the market.
- **Trading:** Stakeholders—alumni, faculty, current students, and even AI agents—are given trading credits to “invest” in the applicants they believe will best achieve the university’s goals.
- **Parallel Evaluation:** Thanks to the capital efficiency of quantum markets, every participant can allocate their full trading power to every applicant they care about, not just a handful.
- **Dynamic Participation:** New applicants or late-breaking information (like a major science fair win) can be added to the market in real time, and the market dynamically adjusts.
- **Wave Function Collapse:** At the end of the process, the market “collapses” to select the cohort of applicants with the highest predicted impact, as determined by the aggregate market predictions.

  

**Why is this novel?**

- **Scalability:** Traditional admissions can’t scale to evaluate tens of thousands of applicants with nuanced, multi-dimensional criteria. Quantum Markets make this possible without requiring more capital or time from evaluators.
- **Incentivized Wisdom of Crowds:** By allowing a broad set of stakeholders to participate, the process leverages diverse perspectives and incentives, potentially surfacing non-obvious candidates.
- **AI Integration:** AI agents could propose, evaluate, and even simulate the long-term impact of different applicant cohorts, making the process more data-driven and less biased.

  

This approach could be extended to other selection problems—like grant funding, startup accelerators, or even hiring—where the option space is vast and the stakes are high. Quantum Markets unlock a fundamentally new way to harness collective intelligence at scale.

Quantum Markets let you use your full capital to evaluate every option in parallel, not just one.

  

Let’s break down how Quantum Markets work, with concrete examples, and why they’re a game-changer for parallel evaluation and capital efficiency—especially in something like college admissions.

  

**How Quantum Markets Work**

  

Traditional decision markets require you to split your capital across all options. If you have $1M and 1,000 proposals, you can only put $1,000 into each if you want to participate everywhere. This makes it hard to have real influence on more than a handful of options.

  

Quantum Markets flip this on its head. When you deposit $1M, you get $1M in trading credits for every proposal—simultaneously. You can use your full capital to express your opinion on any or all proposals, without having to spread yourself thin.

  

**Example: EIP Selection (from the Paradigm article)**

- Suppose there are 700+ Ethereum Improvement Proposals (EIPs).
- In a traditional market, your $1M is split across all 700, so you can only put ~$1,400 per EIP.
- In a Quantum Market, you get $1M in trading credits for each EIP. You can use your full $1M to move the market on EIP 2, and also use your full $1M on EIP 3, etc.
- At the end, only the winning proposal’s market “settles”—the rest are reverted, so your capital isn’t at risk in the losing markets.

  

**Why This Matters for Parallel Evaluation and Capital Efficiency**

  

**Parallel Evaluation**

- **Traditional Markets:** You’re forced to pick a few favorites, because your capital is limited. Most options get ignored, even if you have strong opinions about them.
- **Quantum Markets:** You can act on every option you care about, in parallel, with your full influence. If you think 10 applicants are amazing, you can “bet” on all 10 as if you had your full bankroll for each.

  

**Capital Efficiency**

- **Traditional Markets:** Capital is locked up in every market, so the more options there are, the less impact you have per option.
- **Quantum Markets:** Your capital is “reused” across all options. You don’t need to bring more money to the table as the number of options grows.

  

**College Admissions Example**

  

Imagine a university wants to select the best incoming class from 10,000 applicants.

- **Traditional Market:** If you have $10,000 to allocate, you can only put $1 per applicant if you want to participate everywhere. Your influence is diluted.
- **Quantum Market:** You get $10,000 in trading credits for each applicant. You can use your full $10,000 to “vote” for any applicant you believe in, or even all of them, without spreading yourself thin.
- At the end, only the “winning” applicants (those selected for admission) matter—your capital in the others is returned or reverted.

  

**Why This Is Powerful**

- **Scalability:** You can evaluate thousands of applicants without needing more capital.
- **Expressiveness:** You can support as many applicants as you want, with your full conviction.
- **No Capital Penalty for More Options:** Adding more applicants doesn’t make it harder for you to participate meaningfully.

  

Quantum Markets unlock a fundamentally new way to harness collective intelligence for large-scale, multi-option decisions—making them perfect for things like college admissions, where parallel evaluation and capital efficiency are essential.
