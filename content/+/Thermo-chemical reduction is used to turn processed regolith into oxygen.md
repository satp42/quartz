Reduction is the process by which suitable regolith that has been processed from [[Beneficiation is the process of preparing regolith into suitable feedstock for flowsheet|Beneficiation]] to chemical liberate oxygen that can be captured. There are four promising techniques for [[Lunar regolith can be used for oxygen production in deep-space exploration via flowsheet|Flowsheet oxygen production]] from suitable feedstock of [[Lunar Regolith]]:
1. [[Hydrogen reduction as means of reducing regolith into oxygen]]
2. [[Carbothermal reduction as a means of reducing regolith into oxygen]]
3. [[Molten regolith electrolysis is a means of reducing regolith into oxygen]]
4. [[Molten salt electrolysis is a means of reducing regolith into oxygen]]
Working reactors have been demonstrated to prove that all four techniques are promising in completing reduction. All of these processes require significant energy to be used to heat regolith into high temperatures.
