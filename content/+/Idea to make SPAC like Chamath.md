Below are step-by-step actionable guidelines tailored for a crypto-native launch:

---

## **Step-by-Step Action Plan to Launch “AEXA” in DeFi**

---

## 1. **Define Purpose, Sectors & Investment Thesis**

- Establish that your DAO or SPAC-on-chain will focus on select high-impact sectors (AI, energy, defense, DeFi, etc.), mirroring Chamath’s thesis.
    
- Communicate long-term value creation, not hype or pump schemes.
    

---

## 2. **Design DAO/SPAC Token Structure**

- **Tokenize the “SPAC” fund:** Issue $AEXA governance tokens representing future claim rights and upside.
    
- **No “warrants”:** Omit complex, convoluted instrument structures—keep it single token or clearly separated vesting tokens.
    
- **Sponsor Compensation/Founders’ Alignment:** Align incentives; e.g., founders get their tokens only if the fund succeeds (see “performance tranches” below).
    

---

## 3. **Fundraise in a Fair, Transparent Manner**

- **Oversubscribe & Allocate Carefully:**
    
    - Run a “Fair Launch” or “LBP (Liquidity Bootstrapping Pool)”—institutional participants get priority, but consider small retail allocation (e.g., 1–2% via whitelisting/lottery).
        
    - Cap maximum per-wallet contributions for retail.
        

---

## 4. **Implement Smart Contract Logic for Sponsor/Founder Incentives**

- Use **vesting smart contracts** for founder/sponsor tokens:
    
    - Tokens only become claimable after hitting certain price/TVL/ROI benchmarks.
        
    - **Example:** Founder tokens unlock in three tranches if $AEXA market cap increases 50%, 75%, then 100% (mimic NYSE earnouts).
        

---

## 5. **Launch the DAO-SPAC Treasury**

- Funds are pooled into a transparent on-chain treasury (e.g., Gnosis Safe, Zodiac, or other DAO modules).
    
- Treasury governed by on-chain voting, proposal process (Snapshot, Tally, etc.).
    

---

## 6. **Stipulate Institutional vs. Retail On-chain Allocation**

- Whitelist institutional/private investors or VCs for, say, 98.7% of tokens.
    
- Leave 1.3% for public DeFi participants, perhaps requiring social proof, KYC, or Sybil protections.
    
- Full transparency on allocations and vesting (via block explorer/on-chain analytics).
    

---

## 7. **Establish Target Acquisition/“Go-Public” Parameters**

- Set clear criteria: use DAO voting to identify, due diligence, and select a target protocol, startup, or asset for acquisition/public listing.
    
- Structure the acquisition as a **merger, protocol partnership, or protocol token swap/liquidity event**.
    

---

## 8. **Unruggable Communication & Governance Policy**

- Announce a strict communications/code-of-conduct policy:
    
    - Limit social shilling, clarify incentive structures, minimize public hype—emphasize stewardship rather than pump.
        
    - Document everything—governance forums, Discord, Mirror, or similar.
        

---

## 9. **Listing and Long-term Value Creation**

- Upon “target acquisition,” $AEXA token holders swap their tokens for the new public project’s tokens or receive claim rights, depending on the model.
    
- Treasury and compensation logic remain fully on-chain, auditable, and “if we don’t win together, no one wins.”
    

---

## 10. **Risk & Regulatory Warnings**

- Prominently disclose risk (volatility, regulatory, smart contract) at every stage—remind users that DeFi is “the casino.”
    

---

**Templates and Infrastructure to Use:**

- **Token launch:** ERC-20, OpenZeppelin Governor, Aragon DAO, or DAOHaus.
    
- **Vesting:** Sablier, Superfluid, custom vesting contracts.
    
- **Fundraising:** Balancer LBP, Gnosis Auction, Juicebox, or Mirror Crowdfund.
    
- **Governance:** Snapshot, Tally, Aragon Voting, Compound Governor Bravo.
    
- **Legal:** Consider regulatory analysis for DAO fund structures based on jurisdiction.

**Summary of Chamath Palihapitiya’s “I’m Back” Announcement (AEXA SPAC Launch)**

---

## 1. Launch of American Exceptionalism Acquisition Corp. A (AEXA)

- Chamath announces the launch of American Exceptionalism Acquisition Corp. A, a special purpose acquisition company (SPAC) trading under ticker AEXA on the NYSE.
    
- The SPAC was **over 5x oversubscribed, with $1.4B in demand**, upsizing the offering to $345M.
    
- Focus sectors: **AI, energy, defense, and decentralized finance**—seen as areas for American entrepreneurship and institutional investment.
    

---

## 2. Track Record and Reflections on Past SPACs

- Chamath emphasizes past achievements:
    
    - Helped ignite an asset class that enabled over $150B of private companies to go public in the US.
        
    - **First to raise and price two SPACs in a single day**, reopening markets post-pandemic.
        
    - Offered a new path for entrepreneurs and employees to take companies public efficiently.
        

---

## 3. Lessons Learned & New Structural Reforms in AEXA

- Acknowledges criticism and pitfalls of previous SPACs, especially around **sponsor compensation, forward guidance, and retail investor risk**.
    
- **Key Structural Changes:**
    
    - **No warrants** in the structure.
        
    - **Sponsor compensation is only earned** if the stock appreciates by at least 50% post-combination—paid in three tranches (50%, 75%, 100% stock price increase).
        
- **Block quote:**
    
    > "The sponsor’s founder shares are not earned unless the stock price increases at least 50% following a business combination (or an earlier change of control), at which point there are three tranches of earnings possible - at 50%, 75% and 100% increases in stock price. In other words, if the deal is a dog, no one wins. If it is a winner, we will all win…together."
    

---

## 4. Institutional vs. Retail Investors

- Only **1.3% allocated to retail investors**; the rest (98.7%) is institutional, chosen by Chamath.
    
- Rationale: SPACs are better suited for institutions that can handle volatility and provide long-term support—not typical retail investors.
    
- Warns retail investors to be cautious and review disclosures before participating.
    

---

## 5. Communications Policy

- Chamath will **minimize public commentary and media about AEXA**.
    
- Wants to clarify that these SPACs are not aimed at retail investors and ensure transparency of intent and incentives.
    

---

## 6. Market Opportunity & Rationale for Launching Now

- Points to a growing imbalance: now over 700 private US companies valued above $1B, up from ~150 in 2017.
    
- **Problem:** Employees and early investors have illiquid wealth, making capital recycling and innovation harder.
    
- **Goal:** Use AEXA to bring an exceptional company public, bolstering American innovation and public ownership.
    

---

## 7. Cautions and Closing Remarks

- Emphasizes that **risks remain**; markets are unpredictable.
    
- Ends with a blunt reminder:
    
    > "Markets can go up, but they can also go down. No one can predict what will happen in the future so be safe out there and no crying in the casino."
