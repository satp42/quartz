Centralized governance sucks because of:
- [[The "sky is high, emperor is far away" principle and its erosion]]
- [[Holding accountability for a small group of central leaders is harder]]
- [[Benevolent Dictator Fallacy]]
The issue is that with advancements in AI, data collection, and automation, a singular centralized leader can get a lot more done with less people (interesting connection: [[Y Combinator's goal of the first $100 billion company with 10 people should include decentralized governance into the play]]). This is bad because a lot of potentially catastrophic decisions were stunted because humans-in-the-loop were the last checks that stunted those results.

Additionally because offensive technologies are becoming so easy to use, it has become harder for dissent to revolutionize oppressive regimes. Digitization has made it easy to predict such rebelliousness which leads to suppression of any possible checks. This is why [[Defensive technologies should be emphasized in technological growth]].
