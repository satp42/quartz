[[e/acc]] calls for an overall optimism of technological growth stating that it should be the cornerstone of society. Although that is true, we should be selective of the technologies we should grow and understand the diffrentiation between [[Defensive technology|Defensive Technology vs. Offensive Technology]]. Society's core focus should be to focus on defensive technologies because [[Defensive technologies let societies manage threats through decentralized governance]], [[Defensive technologies expands human freedom and agency]], and produces [[Differential technology development]]

This is the cornerstone of d/acc where the cornerstone focuses are:
- [[Defensive technology|Defense]]
- [[Defensive technologies let societies manage threats through decentralized governance|Decentralization]] which solves [[Coordination problems in governing powerful technologies]]
- [[Defensive technologies let societies manage threats through decentralized governance|Democracy]]
- [[Differential technology development|Differential]]

This is really critical because deferring defensive technology growth often poses critical opportunity cost. For example, looking in the mid-1900s, a lot of the technology with computing we have today would have seemed utopian. With improvements to food, sanitation, medicine, and infrastructure, limiting these growths would actually cost lives. In fact [[Neo-Malthusianism is an incorrect rationale for progress supression]] which led to catastrophic policies like [[China's one child policy]] and massive forced sterilizations in India — but because [[Defensive technologies expands human freedom and agency]], a lot of these calamities could have been stopped.
