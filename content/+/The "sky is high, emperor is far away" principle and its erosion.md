There is a Chinese proverb that says the "sky is high, emperor is far away." This proverb was used to describe how even in centralized government, the core limitation was that the government could not monitor every pocket of society which created buffers of autonomy and independence. However, with the increase of digital authoritarianism, a centralized government could abolish all constraints and monitor every aspect of its society.
