The benefit of [[Proof-of-Work]] is because it stops adversarial attackers from doing Sybil attacks which are when an attacker creates many different accounts to try to maliciously control consensus on the [[Blockchain]]. In PoW, it doesn't matter if the attacker creates many accounts because the attacker doesn't get more processing power.
