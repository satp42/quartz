Hash Functions are a way of doing [[Cryptography]] that takes a "hash function" $H(x)$ such that any arbitrary input for $x$ creates the same fixed-length output of $h$. When they say fixed-length this means that means the length in terms of tokens for the output of $h$ is equivalent no matter the values of $x$.

Hash functions are helpful for blockchain because [[Hash functions use expansion and compression phases to be usable in application]] and [[Hash functions have properties that make them impossible to hack]]
