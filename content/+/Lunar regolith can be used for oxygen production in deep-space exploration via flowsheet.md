[[Lunar Regolith]] will be useful in interplanetary travel because it can be used for oxygen production in deep space. The process of this production, also known as *flowsheet*, is done via three stages: [[In-situ resource extraction is going to be pivotal for deep-space exploration|Extraction]], [[Beneficiation is the process of preparing regolith into suitable feedstock for flowsheet|Beneficiation]], and [[Thermo-chemical reduction is used to turn processed regolith into oxygen|Reduction]]. Of all three of these stages, the least research has been done in Beneficiation. But, primarily the big focus is you need a lot of regolith as an influx in the flowsheet process for oxygen to be effectively released from the material.
