The same way [[RSA encryption]] and [[Elliptic curve cryptography]] use [[Asymmetric key cryptography]] to allow people to securely send messages, we can use this type of cryptography to digitally sign who wrote the message. I can use either [[RSA digital signatures as a way of signing messages securely]] or [[ECDSA]] to prove that a message sent with my private key is verified as sent by me by anyone with my public key.
